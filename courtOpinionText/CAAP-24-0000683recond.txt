(By: Leonard, Presiding Judge, and Wadsworth and McCullen, JJ.) 
Upon consideration of Self-represented Respondent-
Appellant J.M.'s (
Appellant) motion for reconsideration of 
summary disposition order [HRAP Rule 40] (Motion for 
Reconsideration) and motion to supplement the appellate record 
[HRAP Rule 10(e)] (Motion to Supplement), both filed on 
October 12, 2026, and the record, it appears that: 
(1) Appellant seeks reconsideration of this court's 
Summary Disposition Order filed on September 30, 2026, and to 
supplement the record with "ten categories of family court orders and hearing records from related proceedings involving the same parties and the same alleged incidents that form the basis of the Order for Protection affirmed in this case"; 
(2) A motion for reconsideration is not a vehicle for 
arguments that could not have been presented earlier, see Citizens for Equitable & Responsible Gov't v. Cnty. of 
Hawai#i, 
108 Hawai#i 318, 335, 120 P.3d 217, 234 (2005); and NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAII REPORTS OR THE PACIFIC REPORTER 
(3) Appellant presents no point of law or fact that 
this court overlooked or misapprehended. See Hawai# i Rules of 
Appellate Procedure Rule 40(b). 
Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the Motion for 
Reconsideration is denied. The Motion to Supplement is denied as 
moot.
