Tamara  Thompson   (Thompson )  appeals   from  the  October  15,  2024

Appellee   Damien  Wong  (Wong),  after  a  bench  trial,  by  the  District
 
Court  of  the  Third  Circuit,   North  and  South  Kona  Division 
(District   Court).1 
Thompson raises no points of error on appeal, as 
required by Hawai i  Rules of Appellate  Procedure (HRAP) 28(b)(4), 
but contends that the District Court erred by: (1) failing to 
properly weigh evidence of Wong's negligence and its impact on 
her health; (2) ignoring the legal obligations of the landlord

Upon careful review of the record and the briefs 
submitted by the parties, and having given due consideration to 
the arguments advanced and the issues raised, we resolve 
Thompson's contentions as follows. 
Thompson does not cite to where in the record the 
alleged errors occurred or where in the record she objected or 
brought the alleged errors to the District Court's attention. 
See HRAP Rule 28(b)(4). Thompson did not provide this court with 
transcripts of the trial proceedings. See State v. Hoang, 93 
Hawai i 333,  336, 3  P.3d 499, 502 (2002) (without transcripts, 
there is an insufficient record to review alleged trial court 
error); see also HRAP
 Rule 10(b). 
Thompson fails to cite to the record, specifically or 
even generally, to support her claims of improper weighing of 
evidence, disregard of the law, and/or bias. On this record, we 
conclude that Thompson's contentions provide no basis for relief 
on appeal. 
Accordingly,  the  District  Court's  October  15,  2024 
Judgment  is  affirmed.
