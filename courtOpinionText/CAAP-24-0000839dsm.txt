ORDER DISMISSING APPEAL    
(By: Leonard, Presiding Judge, Wadsworth and Gluck, JJ.) 
Upon review of the record, it appears that: 
(1) On October 5, 2026, the court ordered that within 
ten days from the order, self-represented Petitioner-Appellant River Young (Young) shall either pay the filing fee in the full amount to the Supreme Court Clerk's Office, or file a motion for leave to proceed on appeal 
in forma pauperis that complies with 
Hawaiʻi Rules of Appellate Procedure (HRAP) Rule 24(a). The 
court cautioned Young that failure to comply with the order may result in the appeal being dismissed; and   NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAIʻI REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
 
   
 
  
 
  
 (2) Young did not respond to the October 5, 2026 order 
and has not paid the filing fee or obtained a fee waiver for 
this appeal, resulting in no record on appeal being filed. An appeal may be dismissed where the record on appeal has not been prepared because the appellant failed to pay the required fees or obtain an order allowing the appellant to proceed in forma pauperis. HRAP Rule 11(b)(2), (c)(2). 
Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the appeal is 
dismissed.
