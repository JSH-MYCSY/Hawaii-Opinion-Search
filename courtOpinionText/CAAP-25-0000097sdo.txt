"The preliminary question of whether a covenant is 
ambiguous is a question of law that may be resolved on summary 
judgment." Hiner, 90 Hawai# i at 190, 977 P.2d at 880. A 
restrictive covenant "is ambiguous when its terms are reasonably susceptible to more than one meaning." Moloaa Farms LLC v. Green 
Energy Team LLC, 157 Hawai# i 175, 189, 575 P.3d 808, 822 (2025). 
We conclude CC&R paragraphs 1.13 and 4.1(b), read 
together, are ambiguous because they are reasonably susceptible to more than one meaning. Giacometti and Tius planting trees and building a fence in the setback area could be both prohibited and allowed, even without Design Committee approval, under the CC&R in effect when the Wagners' lawsuit was filed. Accordingly, we conclude the ambiguous restrictive covenants at issue are unenforceable against Giacometti and Tius under the circumstances of this case.
