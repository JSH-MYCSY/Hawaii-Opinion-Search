Upon careful review of the record, briefs, and 
relevant legal authorities, and having given due consideration 
to the arguments advanced and the issues raised by the parties, 
we affirm the Order Denying Motion to Vacate entered in favor of 
Plaintiff -Appellee Wilmington Savings Fund Society, FSB, D/B/A 
Christiana Trust as Owner Trustee of the Residential Credit 
Opportunities Trust III ( Wilmington Savings ). 
Kekaualua raises seven points of error on appeal, 
contending that the circuit court erred b ecause: (1) it "denied 
the ["Motion to Vacate Pursuant to HRCP Rule 60(b)(4)" ( Motion 
to Vacate )] despite the [Temporary Restraining Order ( TRO)] 
acknowledging 'title is in dispute'"; (2) it "improperly relied 
on unsworn counsel declarations as evidence"; (3) "[e]quity 
jurisdiction was not applied to halt ejectment where title was 
unsettled"; (4) "[f]iduciary duties owed to Native Hawaiian 
rights and lands were disregarded"; (5) "[Wilmington Savings] 
failed to prove superior title due to deed defects"; (6) it 
"ignored unrebutted affidavits"; and (7) "[t]he ejectment caused 
wrongful injuries and deprivation of rights ."2
