Upon review of the record, it appears that: 
(a) Self-represented Petitioner-Appellant Rocky 
Hermanns-Raymond (Hermanns-Raymond ) appeals from the April 21, 
2025 "Court's Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Order 
Denying Rule 40 Petition" entered by the Circuit Court of the 
Second Circuit. 
(b) Hermanns-Raymond did not file the statement of 
jurisdiction or opening brief and has not taken any further 
action in this appeal. Hermanns-Raymond's address for service of 
process in this appeal is the Halawa Correctional Facility. 
(c) The appellate clerk served Hermanns-Raymond with a 
copy of the December 30, 2025 order, which was returned to the 
appellate clerk on January 12, 2026 via U.S. mail with a label 
stating "Return to Sender[,]" "Attempted – Not Known[,]" and 
"Unable to Forward[,]" and a checkmark to indicate "Not Here[.]" 
(d) The
 court's September 30, 2026 order instructed 
Respondent-Appellee State of Hawai i to file a declaration          NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAI I REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
       
    
      
          
       
        
          
           
        
      
         
  
        
           
        
        
 indicating Hermanns-Raymond's custody status, and his current or 
last known location and address. 
(e) Deputy prosecuting attorney Gerald K. Enriques 
filed a declaration that stated "[t]o the best of the State's 
knowledge, Hermanns-Raymond was reported as deceased while in 
custody at Halawa Correctional Facility on December 19, 2025." 
The declaration attached a copy of a December 20, 2025 "reporting 
of a death" from the Director of the State of Hawai i Department 
of Corrections and

the department's public website, that indicates "Hermanns-
Raymond, Rocky M.K." died on December 19, 2025 at Halawa 
Correctional Facility. 
Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the appeal is 
dismissed as moot. See State v. Makaila , 79 Hawai i  40, 45  & 
n.11, 897 P.2d 967, 972 & n.11 (1995).
