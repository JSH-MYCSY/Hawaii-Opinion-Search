Upon review of the record, it appears that: 
(1) On February 5, 2026, self-represented Respondent-
Appellant J.H. filed the notice of appeal in this case; 
(2)
 On February 18, 2026, the family court clerk 
notified J.H. that J.H.'s request to proceed in forma pauperis was denied and the filing fees needed to be paid, and cautioned J.H. that failure to pay the fees may result in the appeal being 
dismissed; 
(3) The record on appeal was due on or before April 6, 
2026, see Hawaiʻ i Rules of Appellate Procedure (HRAP)   NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAIʻI REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
 
  
  
 
  
 
  
 
  
 
  
 
  
 
 Rule 11(b)(1), but was not filed because J.H. failed to pay the 
filing fees or obtain a fee waiver; 
(4) On May 14, 2026, the appellate clerk entered a 
default of the record on appeal; 
(5) On June 29, 2026, the court entered an order 
granting a June 8, 2026 motion by J.H. seeking relief from default of the record on appeal and additional time to pay the filing fees for this appeal; 
(6) The June 29, 2026 order extended the deadline for 
J.H. to pay the filing fees in this appeal to July 29, 2026, and cautioned that failure to comply with the order may result in dismissal of this appeal; and 
(7) J.H. has not taken any further action in this 
appeal. An appeal may be dismissed where the record on appeal has not been prepared because the appellant failed to pay the required fees or obtain an order allowing the appellant to proceed in forma pauperis. HRAP Rule 11(b)(2), (c)(2). 
Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the appeal is 
dismissed.
