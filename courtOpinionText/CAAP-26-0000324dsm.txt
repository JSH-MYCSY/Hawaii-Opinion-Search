 ORDER DISMISSING APPEAL    
(By: Leonard, Presiding Judge, Guidry and Gluck, JJ.) 
Upon review of the record, it appears that: 
(1) The opening brief was due on or before July 7, 
2026; 
(2) Self-represented Defendant/Counterclaimant-
Appellant Alexander Y. Marn (Alexander Marn) failed to file the opening brief or request a further extension of time; 
(3) On July 13, 2026, the appellate clerk entered a 
default notice informing Alexander Marn that the time for filing the opening brief had expired, the matter would be called to the court's attention on July 23, 2026, for appropriate action, 
which could include dismissal of the appeal, under Hawaiʻ i Rules 
of Appellate Procedure (HRAP) Rule 30, and Alexander Marn could 
request relief from default by motion; 
(4) The appellate clerk electronically served the 
notice of entering case on calendar and the default notice upon Alexander Marn; and 
(5) Alexander Marn has not taken further action in 
this appeal.   Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the appeal is 
dismissed.
