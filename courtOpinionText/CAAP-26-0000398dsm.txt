 APPEAL FROM THE CIRCUIT COURT OF THE SECOND CIRCUIT 
(CASE NO. 2CC191000312) 
ORDER DISMISSING APPEAL    
(By: Wadsworth, Presiding Judge, McCullen and Gluck, JJ.) 
Upon review of the record, it appears that: 
(1) On May 21, 2026, self-represented Defendant-
Appellant Rosemond K. Pettigrew (Pettigrew) filed a notice of appeal. 
(2) On August 27, 2026, the appellate clerk entered a 
"Default of Opening Brief," which informed Pettigrew that the time for filing the opening brief expired on August 24, 2026, the matter would be called to the court's attention on September 8, 2026 for action that may include dismissal of the 
appeal under Hawaiʻ i Rules of Appellate Procedure Rule 30, and 
Pettigrew could request relief from default by motion. 
(3) Pettigrew failed to file the opening brief, did 
not request relief from default, and has not taken any further 
action in this appeal. 
Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the appeal is 
dismissed. 
IT IS FURTHER ORDERED that all pending motions are 
dismissed.
