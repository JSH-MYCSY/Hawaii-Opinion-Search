(By: Hiraoka, Presiding Judge, Wadsworth and Gluck, JJ.) 
Upon review of the record, it appears that: 
(1)
 On June 3, 2026, Petitioner-Appellant Gabriel 
Adviento, Jr. (Adviento
), filed the notice of appeal in this 
case; 
(2) On June 16, 2026, the appellate clerk notified 
Adviento's counsel, Jamae K.K. Kawauchi, that the filing fees had not been paid, and cautioned that failure to pay the fees or request a waiver may result in the appeal being dismissed or the notice of appeal being stricken;   NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAIʻI REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
 
   (3) The record on appeal was due on or before  
August 3, 2026. See  Hawaiʻi Rules of Appellate Procedure (HRAP)  
Rule 11(b)(1);  
  
 
  
 
  
 
 
 
 
 (4) On August 10, 2026, the appellate clerk entered a 
default of the record on appeal, informing Adviento's counsel 
that the time to docket the appeal had expired, counsel had not paid the filing fees or obtained an order allowing Adviento to proceed on appeal in forma pauperis, the matter would be brought to the court's attention on August 20, 2026, for action that may include dismissal of the appeal, and Adviento could seek relief from default by motion; and 
(5) Adviento has not taken any further action in this 
appeal. 
An appeal may be dismissed where the record on appeal 
has not been prepared because the appellant failed to pay the required fees or obtain an order allowing the appellant to proceed in forma pauperis. HRAP Rule 11(b)(2), (c)(2). 
Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the appeal is 
dismissed.
