Upon review of the record, it appears that: 
(1) The statement of jurisdiction and opening brief 
were due on or before August 17, 2026, and September 14, 2026, 
respectively; 
(2) Self-represented Plaintiffs-Appellants Akaka Falls 
Farm LLC (Akaka Falls Farm), Lunakanawai Hauanio (Hauanio ) and 
Margaret M. Connor (Connor) failed to file either document or 
request an extension of time; 
(3) On September 21, 2026, the appellate clerk entered 
a default notice informing Akaka Falls Farm, Hauanio and Connor 
that the time for filing the statement of jurisdiction and 
opening brief had expired, the matter would be called to the 
court's attention on October 1, 2026, for appropriate action,          NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAI I REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER

 12.1(e) and 30, and Akaka Falls

motion; and 
(4) Akaka Falls Farm,1 Hauanio and Connor have not 
taken any further action in this appeal. 
Therefore,  IT  IS  HEREBY  ORDERED  that  the   appeal  is 
dismissed.

1 As a limited liability company, Akaka Falls Farm must be represented 
by legal counsel in litigation. See Hawai i Revi sed Statutes (HRS) § 605-2 
(2016) & HRS § 605-14 (2016); Oahu Plumbing & Sheet Metal, Ltd. v. Kona 
Const., Inc., 60 Haw. 372, 377, 590 P.2d 570, 573-74 (1979) ("Non-attorney 
agents are not allowed to represent corporations in litigation . . . ."); 
Kailua 50 LLC v. Ichida Kemp, No. CAAP-19-0000799, 2020 WL 429323, at *1 (Haw. 
App. Jan. 28, 2020) (SDO) (holding a non-attorney is not allowed to represent 
a limited liability company in litigation). Accordingly, Akaka Falls Farm 
could not proceed in this appeal unless represented by legal counsel.
