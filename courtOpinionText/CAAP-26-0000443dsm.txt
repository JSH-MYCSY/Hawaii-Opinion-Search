Upon review of the record, it appears that: 
(1) On June 10, 2026, self -represented Plaintiff -
Appellant Christopher Bucknell ( Bucknell ) filed the notice of 
appeal in this case; 
(2) The record on appeal was due on or before 
August 10, 2026, see Hawaiʻi Rules of Appellate Procedure ( HRAP) 
Rule 11(b)(1), but was not filed because Bucknell failed to pay 
the filing fees or obtain a fee waiver; 
(3) On August 11, 2026, the appellate clerk entered a 
default of the record on appeal, informing Bucknell that the 
time to docket the appeal had expired, Bucknell had not paid the 
filing fees or obtained a fee waiver, the matter would be   NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAI‘I REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER

(4) On August 15, 2026, Bucknell filed a motion

time to pay the filing fees for this appeal, and, in the 
alternative, leave to proceed on appeal in forma pauperis ; 
(5) By order entered October 22, 2026, the court
