Upon consideration of Petitioner-
Appellees Carroll S. 
Taylor and Paul R. Cassiday, Jr.'s Motion to Dismiss Appeal filed 
on September 3, 2026, the papers in support and in opposition, 
and the record, it appears that: 
(1) The Circuit Court of the First Circuit entered the 
Judgment Pursuant to Findings of Fact, Conclusions of Law, and 
Order Granting Petition for Approval of Final Accounts for the 
Period January 1, 2022 Through May 31, 2025; for Approval of Termination and Special Service Fees; and for Final Release and Discharge of Successor Co-Trustees [Dkt. 1788] on June 24, 2026; 
(2) Beneficiary Carol Cassiday Orr's Notice of Appeal 
from the Judgment was filed on July 27, 2026; 
(3) Appellees seek dismissal of Orr's appeal because 
her Notice of Appeal was not "filed within 30 days after entry of the judgment" as required by Hawai# i Rules of Appellate Procedure 
(HRAP
) Rule 4(a)(1); 
(4) Under HRAP Rule 26(a), "the day of the . . . event 
. . . from which the designated period of time begins to run NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAI #I REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
shall not be included." Thus, "one day after entry" of the 
June 24, 2026 Judgment excludes June 24, and would be June 25, 2026; thirty days after June 24, 2026, is July 24, 2026; 
(5) Orr's reliance on Wilmington Sav. Fund Soc'y, FSB 
v. Ryan, 148 Hawai# i 515, 479 P.3d 133 (2021) is misplaced 
because Orr did not move the circuit court to extend the time for filing the notice of appeal within thirty days after July 24, 2026, see HRAP Rule 4(a)(4)(B); and 
(6) Compliance with the requirement of timely filing a 
notice of appeal is jurisdictional. Grattafiori v. State, 79 
Hawai#i 10, 13, 897 P.2d 937, 940 (1995). 
Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that Appellees' Motion 
to Dismiss Appeal is granted. This appeal is dismissed for lack of jurisdiction.
