ORDER APPROVING STIPULATION FOR DISMISSAL OF APPEAL   
(By: Nakasone, Chief Judge, Wadsworth and Gluck, JJ.) 
Upon consideration of the Stipulation for Dismissal of 
Appeal (Stipulation), filed October 23, 2026, by Defendant-
Appellant Abraham Korn, M.D., the papers in support, and the record, it appears that: 
(1) Under Hawaiʻ i Rules of Appellate Procedure (HRAP) 
Rule 42(b), the parties stipulate to dismiss the appeal and bear their own costs of appeal; 
(2) The Stipulation is dated and signed by counsel for 
all appearing parties; 
(3) The appeal has not been docketed; and   NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST'S HAWAIʻI REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
 
  
 
 
  
   
   
 
 
 (4) HRAP Rule 42(b) governs dismissal of docketed 
appeals, whereas HRAP Rule 42(a) authorizes dismissal before an 
appeal is docketed. 
Therefore, IT IS HEREBY ORDERED that the Stipulation 
is approved and the appeal is dismissed, pursuant to HRAP Rule 42(a).  The parties shall bear their own costs of appeal.
