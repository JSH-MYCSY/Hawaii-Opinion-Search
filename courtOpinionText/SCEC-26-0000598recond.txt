Upon consideration of Defendant Sheila Medeiros’ s  
Motion for Reconsideration,  filed September 9, 2026, which seeks 
reconsideration of this court’s September 4, 2026 Findings of 
Fact, Conclusions of Law, and Judgment,  Defendant Medeiros has   
  
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
  
  
 
  
 
 not established points of law or fact that this court has

Procedure Rule 40(b) (eff. 2000).

denied.

DISSENT BY McKENNA, ACTING C.J. AND EDDINS, J. 
We would grant Sheila Medeiros’s Motion for 
Reconsideration in part. For the reasons set out in our amended 
separate opinion to be filed tomorrow, we would vacate Judgment 
paragraph A of the September 4, 2026 Findings of Fact, 
Conclusions of Law, and Judgment, and complete the primary after 
the November 3, 2026 general election, with the winner deemed 
elected under Article III, Section 4. We would deny the motion 
in all other respects.
