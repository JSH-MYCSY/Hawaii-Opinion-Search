ORDER DENYING PETITION FOR WRIT OF MANDAMUS   
(By: Devens, C.J., McKenna, Eddins, and Ginoza, JJ., 
and Circuit Judge Hamman, assigned by reason of vacancy) 
Upon consideration of the petition for writ of mandamus 
filed July 14, 2025, and the record, Petitioner has not 
demonstrated a clear and indisputable right to relief, nor a lack of alternative means to seek the relief sought in this original proceeding. An extraordinary writ is unwarranted. See 
Womble Bond Dickinson (US) LLP v. Kim, 153 Hawaiʻ i 307, 319, 537 
P.3d 1154, 1166 (2023). 
It is ordered that the petition is denied.
