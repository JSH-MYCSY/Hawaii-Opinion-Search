which we construe as a petition for extraordinary writ, and the

right to relief, nor a lack of alternatives to obtain the relief 
sought. See Womble Bond Dickinson (US) LLP v. Kim, 153 Hawaiʻ i 
307, 319, 537 P.3d 1154, 1166 (2023); see also Hawaiʻ i Rules of 
Penal Procedure Rule 40 (eff. 2006). 
It is ordered that the petition is denied.
