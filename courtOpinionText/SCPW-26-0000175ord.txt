ORIGINAL PROCEEDING  
(CASE NO. 1CCV-24-0001303)  
ORDER DENYING PETITION FOR WRIT OF MANDAMUS  
(By: McKenna, Acting C.J., Eddins, and Ginoza, JJ., 
and Circuit Judge Cahill , in place of Devens, C.J., recused, 
and Circuit Judge Kubota, assigned by reason of vacancy ) 
Upon consideration of the petition for writ of mandamus and 
prohibition filed March 10, 2026, and the record, Petitioner’s 
request for a writ that requires the clerk to file a motion is moot because the motion was filed in the circuit court on 
June 2, 2026. Also, Petitioner’s concerns about the timeliness 
of a notice of appeal and requests to enter the appellate   
    
 
  
     
       
       mediation program are more appropriately addressed on appeal.     
An extraordinary writ is unwarranted.   See Barnett v. Broderick , 
84 Hawaiʻ i 109, 111, 929 P.2d 1359, 1361 (1996);  see also Salling  
v. Moon, 76 Hawai
ʻi 273, 274 n.3, 874 P.2d 1098, 1099 n.3 (1994).  
It is ordered that the petition is denied.
