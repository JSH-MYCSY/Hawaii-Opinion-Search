ORDER DENYING PETITIONS  
(By: Devens, C.J., McKenna, Eddins, and Ginoza, JJ., 
and Circuit Judge Bernard Lee, assigned by reason of vacancy ) 
On March 17, 2026, three documents from Francis Grandinetti 
(Petitioner) were filed in this court and titled: (1) “HRAP 
Agency Injunction Application and Agency Mandamus Application: 
Police-Corruption and ‘Ticket-fixing’ case”; (2) “Injunction 
Application”; and (3) “On Mandamus Application to Vacate, Void, 
Reverse, or Remand Hilo DCTC Case with Judge J. Hawk; February 
8-10, 2025 arrest case.”  We construe these documents as 
petitions for extraordinary writ.  
Upon consideration of the petitions and the record, an 
extraordinary writ is unwarranted because Petitioner has not 
demonstrated a clear and indisputable right to relief, nor a  
  
 
  
 
 
    
       
       lack of alternative means to obtain the relief sought.   See  
Womble Bond Dickinson (US) LLP v. Kim      , 153 Hawaiʻ i 307, 319, 537 
P.3d 1154, 1166 (2023).  
It is ordered that the petitions are denied. 
It is also ordered: 
1. We construe docket 14 as a motion and deny the motion. 
2. The clerk shall file a redacted version of docket 20 
because docket 20 contains personal information (full account 
number) that should have been redacted pursuant to Rules 2.19 
and 9 of the Hawaiʻ i Court Records Rules.
