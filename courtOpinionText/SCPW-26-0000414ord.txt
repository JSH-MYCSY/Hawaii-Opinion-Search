ORIGINAL PROCEEDING 
ORDER DENYING PETITION 
(By: Devens , C.J., Eddins, and Ginoza, JJ., and 
Circuit Judge Ashford, in place of McKenna, J., recused, 
and Circuit Judge Char, assigned by reason of vacancy) 
Upon consideration of the petition for writ of mandamus and 
habeas corpus, filed June 4, 2026, and the record, Petitioner 
has not demonstrated a clear and indisputable right to relief. 
An extraordinary writ is unwarranted. See Womble Bond Dickinson
(US) LLP v. Kim, 153 Hawaiʻi 307, 319, 537 P.3d 1154, 1166 
(2023). It is ordered: 
1. The petition is denied. See Haw. Const. art. VI, § 2 
(authorizing the chief justice to assign a judge of the 
intermediate appellate court or a circuit court to serve 
temporarily on the supreme court). See also Hawaiʻi Revised    
  
  
 
 
        
        
        
 Statutes § 660-3 (2016) (providing the basis for when the 
supreme court may issue writs of habeas corpus); and 
2. The filing fee is waived.
