ORDER DENYING PETITIONS WITHOUT PREJUDICE    
(By: Devens, C.J., McKenna,  Eddins,  and Ginoza,  JJ.,  
and Circuit Judge Medeiros, assigned by reason of vacancy )  
Letters from Petitioner were filed in this court on July 6 
and August 5, 2026.  We construe these letters as petitions for 
extraordinary writ. Upon consideration of these petitions and 
the record, Petitioner has not demonstrated a clear and 
indisputable right to relief nor a lack of alternatives to obtain the relief sought. See Womble Bond Dickinson (US) LLP v. 
Kim (Womble), 153 Hawaiʻ i 307, 319, 537 P.3d 1154, 1166 (2023).  
Post-conviction relief should instead be first pursued by filing a petition in the circuit court pursuant to Rule 40 of the 
Hawaiʻi Rules of 
Penal Procedure (HRPP).   
  
 
  
        
       It is ordered that the petitions are denied without 
prejudice to filing a HRPP Rule 40 petition in the circuit 
court.
