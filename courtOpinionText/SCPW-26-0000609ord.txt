ORDER DENYING PETITION FOR WRITS OF PROHIBITION AND MANDAMUS   
(By: Devens, C.J., McKenna, Eddins, and Ginoza, JJ., 
and Circuit Judge Copeland, assigned by reason of vacancy ) 
Upon consideration of the petition for writs of prohibition 
and mandamus filed August 27, 2026, and the record, Petitioners 
have not demonstrated a clear and indisputable right to relief nor a lack of alternative means to obtain the relief sought. An extraordinary writ is unwarranted. See Womble Bond Dickinson 
(US) LLP v. Kim, 153 Hawaiʻ i 307, 319, 537 P.3d 1154, 1166 
(2023). 
It is ordered that the petition is denied.
