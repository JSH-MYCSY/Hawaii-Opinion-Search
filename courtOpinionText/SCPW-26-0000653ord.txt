ORDER DENYING PETITION FOR WRIT OF MANDAMUS AND/OR PROHIBITION 
(By: Devens , C.J., McKenna, Eddins, and Ginoza, JJ., 
and Circuit Judge Souza , assigned by reason of vacancy)  
Upon consideration of the petition for writ of mandamus 
and/or prohibition filed September 16, 2026, and the record, an 
extraordinary writ is not intended to be used to control the 
exercise of a judge’s discretion before the judge has issued a 
decision on the issues before the circuit court.  Naipo v. 
Border, 125 Hawaiʻi 31, 34, 251 P.3d 594, 597 (2011).  Following 
the judge’s decision on counsel representation, Petitioner may 
seek review of that decision through normal appellate 
procedures, if and when appropriate.  An extraordinary writ is unwarranted.  See Womble Bond Dickinson (US) LLP v. Kim, 153 
Hawaiʻi 307, 319, 537 P.3d 1154, 1166 (2023).    Electronically Filed
