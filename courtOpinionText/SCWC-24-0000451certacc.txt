Pursuant to this court’s discretion set forth in Hawaiʻ i 
Revised Statutes § 602-59(a), the court accepts 
Petitioner/Defendant-Appellant Lawrence Turno’s application for writ of certiorari filed on July 21, 2026. 
Under Hawaiʻ i Rules of Appellate Procedure (HRAP) Rule 40.1, 
this court “may limit the question on review.” Here, we limit 
the questions on review to questions presented “2” and “3” of 
Petitioner’s application for writ of certiorari.   
 
  
 
      
         
        
        
        It is further ordered, that no oral argument will be heard 
in this case. Any party may, within ten days and pursuant to HRAP Rule 34(c), move for retention of oral argument.
