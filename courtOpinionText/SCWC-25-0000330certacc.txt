(By: Devens, C.J., McKenna, and Eddins, JJ., and 
Circuit Judge Remigio, assigned by reason of vacancy;
and Ginoza, J., dissenting) 
Pursuant to this court’s discretion set forth in Hawaiʻ i 
Revised Statutes § 602-59(a), the court accepts 
Petitioner/Plaintiff-Appellant P.C.’s application for writ of 
certiorari filed on August 13, 2026, deemed timely filed as of 
September 1, 2026. 
Under Hawaiʻ i Rules of Appellate Procedure (HRAP) Rule 40.1, 
this court “may limit the question on review.” 
Here, we limit  2 
  
    
   
    
         
        
        
 
 
 
 
 
 
        the question on review to the second question presented in  
Petitioner’s application for writ of certiorari:  
Whether the ICA gravely erred in holding that HRS §  571-
46(a)(9) was properly applied and  “effectively rebutted” 
after the Family Court found Father committed family 
violence against Mother, where the statute makes safety and 
well-being primary and raises a rebuttable presumption 
against awarding custody to a perpetrator of family violence.  
It is further ordered that no oral argument will be heard  
in this case. Any party may, within ten days and pursuant  
to HRAP Rule 34(c), move for retention of oral argument.  
DATED: Honolulu, Hawaiʻ i, October 29, 2026.
