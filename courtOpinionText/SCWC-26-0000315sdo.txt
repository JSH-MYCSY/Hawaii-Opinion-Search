We review the denial of a motion to proceed in forma 
pauperis for abuse of discretion.  Estate Admin. Servs., LLC v. 
Mohulamu, 148 10, 16, 466 P.3d 408, 414 (2020); Blaisdell 
v. Dep’t of Pub. Safety, 113  315, 319, 151 P.3d 796, 800 
(2007).  An abuse of discretion occurs when a court “clearly 
exceed[s] the bounds of reason or disregard[s] rules or 
principles of law or practice to the substantial detriment of a 
party litigant.”  Mohulamu, 148  at 16, 466 P.3d at 414.  
HRS § 607-3 gives courts “discretionary power to waive the HawaiʻiHawaiʻiHawaiʻi NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST’S HAWAIʻ I REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
 
4 prepayment of costs or to reduce or remit costs where, in 
special or extraordinary cases, the cost of any suit, action, or 
proceeding may, to the judges, appear onerous.”  “Onerous” means 
“[e]xcessively burdensome or troublesome; causing hardship[.]”  
Blaisdell, 113 Hawaiʻi at 319, 151 P.3d at 800 (quoting Black’s 
Law Dictionary 1122 (8th ed. 2004)).  Hawaiʻi Rules of Appellate 
Procedure (HRAP) Rule 24(a) (eff. 2020) requires a party seeking to proceed in forma pauperis on appeal to submit a declaration 
showing “the party’s inability to pay the required filing fees 
or to give security for costs.”  This court has “long adhered to 
the policy of affording litigants the opportunity to be heard on 
the merits whenever possible,” recognizing that denial of in 
forma pauperis status “affects access to justice in civil 
matters for those unable to afford legal representation.”  
Mohulamu, 148 
Hawaiʻi at 18, 466 P.3d at 416.  
The ICA’s orders did not address Hawkes’ unrebutted 
representation that his total liquid net worth across all bank 
accounts was $91.61.  Nor did the ICA explain why $4,000.00 in reported gross monthly income outweighed that showing, or weigh 
Hawkes’ income against his living costs.  The ICA instead again 
ordered Hawkes to pay the full $315.00, more than three times 
his total liquid assets, within ten days, and denied his motion 
for an emergency stay of that deadline.  Even accepting 
Respondents’ contention that Hawkes’ filings reflected NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST’S HAWAIʻ I REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
 
5 approximately $853.00 in monthly disposable income, nothing in 
the record indicates that Hawkes had funds available to pay the 
fees within the time the ICA allowed.  On this record, the only 
reasonable conclusion is that requiring Hawkes to prepay the 
filing fees would be “onerous” within the meaning of HRS § 607-
3. 
The ability to pay for purposes of HRS § 607-3 and 
HRAP Rule 24 turns on more than an applicant’s gross income.  
See Blaisdell, 113 Hawaiʻ i at 319, 151 P.3d at 800 (noting that 
“when considering a person’s ability to pay, the court may 
consider the size of the movant’s estate and his or her net 
worth” (quoting Minatoya v. Mousel, 2 Haw. App. 1, 8, 625 P.2d 
378, 384 (1981))).  Because the ICA’s orders did not address 
Hawkes’ net financial position or his cost of living, the ICA’s 
rulings denying in forma pauperis status, denying 
reconsideration, and dismissing Hawkes’ appeal for nonpayment 
were unreasonable.  Consistent with this court’s policy of 
resolving appeals on the merits whenever possible, Mohulamu, 148 
Hawaiʻi at 18, 466 P.3d at 416, we hold that the ICA abused its 
discretion in denying Hawkes’ motion for leave to proceed in 
forma pauperis and, in turn, in dismissing his appeal.  
Accordingly, we vacate the ICA’s June 24, 2026, order 
denying Hawkes’ motion for leave to proceed in forma pauperis, its June 26, 2026, order denying reconsideration of that order, NOT FOR PUBLICATION IN WEST’S HAWAIʻ I REPORTS AND PACIFIC REPORTER 
 
6 its July 10, 2026, order dismissing CAAP-26-0000315 for 
nonpayment of filing fees, and its July 15, 2026, order denying 
Hawkes’ motion for reconsideration and relief from default.  
This case is remanded to the ICA with instructions to grant 
Hawkes’ motion for leave to proceed in forma pauperis and to 
reinstate the appeal.  
Hawkes’ pending requests for a stay of the ICA’s fee 
payment deadline are denied as moot.
